# Individual diagnostic panels

Each PDF isolates one comparison across mixing 0, 0.5, and 1. Input panels show the BH reservoirs; yield panels count terminal mergers; chain panels describe repeated mergers. Increasing mixing shifts the terminal median remnant mass from 314 to 105 to 85 solar masses. Recorded chain events fall from 24.06 to 13.70 to 4.24 million, mainly through shorter nuclear recycling histories. Terminal counts change much less. These are individual stochastic runs; chain records omit some recoil-ending events, and extreme masses can reach the imposed growth limit.

## Top three results

Results below follow mixing **0 → 0.5 → 1**.

1. **Lower remnant masses:** median terminal remnant mass falls **314 → 105 → 85 solar masses**. [Mass comparison](03_terminal_remnant.pdf).
2. **Less nuclear recycling:** recorded nuclear chain events fall **23.94 → 13.60 → 4.16 million**, while nuclear terminal yields stay near **89–93%**. Mixing changes repeated growth much more than terminal merger production. [Event yields](02_yields_recorded_events_per_candidate.pdf).
3. **Rare long chains dominate:** the longest **1%** of recorded chains supply **99.42% → 99.15% → 97.75%** of all recorded events; median chain length is **one** in every run. [Chain lengths](08_per_chain_length.pdf).

Start with [terminal yield](02_yields_terminal_yield.pdf), [terminal remnant masses](03_terminal_remnant.pdf), [recorded event counts](02_yields_recorded_event_count.pdf), and [chain lengths](08_per_chain_length.pdf).
